# Independent facility and project evidence

Compute Atlas serves utilities, grid operators, developers, planners and researchers. No utility's service territory determines national scope. Nationwide scope does not establish complete coverage.

## Record identity

Distinguish campus, building and development phase. Record aliases and suspected duplicate relationships without merging them automatically. A matching company and city is insufficient to assign a phase to an inherited coordinate. Unresolved source-backed projects remain in the register without map markers; do not count them as additional campuses.

## Lifecycle and evidence

Maintain separate values for announcement, application, approval, construction, operation, cancellation and withdrawal. Each claim needs an exact phase/scope, primary source, publication/update date, status-as-of date and review date. A company statement is operator-reported evidence, not independent site verification. A target opening is not an approved energization date. Older claims are not silently promoted to current status.

Preserve inherited status separately. The source-backed register expands incrementally with dated milestones, not a complete US pipeline. Two inherited Wisconsin entries need reconciliation with Microsoft's first operating facility and second construction phase. Existing status and coordinates are not overwritten by this review.

## Resource definitions

IT load, nameplate electrical capacity, measured peak, requested connection and annual energy are different quantities. Every accepted MW/MWh claim needs a definition, reporting period and source. Do not aggregate inherited capacities. Water withdrawals and consumption must retain their reporting-location scope to avoid counting one disclosure repeatedly.

## Network relationships

Historical polygon intersection is context, not a confirmed supplier or grid connection. Confirm utility, connection point, study stage and network upgrades from dated project-specific documents. Nearby infrastructure is not supply evidence. Source, validation, retrieval and review dates remain separate.

## Export and review

Exports carry the same caveats, claim scope and sources as the interface. Missing fields remain null/unknown. Filters do not establish completeness. Source-backed phases cannot be presented as independently verified facilities. Corrections need a public source and a recorded review; no automatic refresh cadence is promised.

## Completed desk reviews

Every inherited future-project record has an initial review disposition in `src/data/future-project-reviews.json`. Review completion does not verify current status, phase identity, coordinates, demand or utility. A source about another place, an office, a lab, a cable landing, or a state-wide investment is context only. Failed search is not proof of absence. Undated source observations do not create opening dates. Municipal staff recommendations are not final approvals. Source-note coverage and completed desk-review coverage are separate counts.

## Business identity and ownership

`public/facility-identities.json` stores selected source-reported campus and project relationships separately from inherited inventory names/operators. Every profile carries a primary URL, publication date when supplied, review date, association scope and role-specific explanations. Candidate links do not verify parcel or building identities. Do not infer legal ownership from an operator brand, corporate parent, hosting customer or data-center end user. A future legal-owner claim requires a dated parcel/title record and documented corporate linkage. Company-name matching alone cannot fill missing operators. Hosting customers are not automatically building tenants. These limits travel with register and comparison exports.
